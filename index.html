About

Researcher, builder, perpetual student.

I'm a Senior Machine Learning Researcher at Roblox, working on 3D/4D generative AI, content understanding, and agentic AI.

I did my PhD at the University of Maryland with Dr. Jordan Boyd-Graber, working on human-centered NLP: interactive topic modeling and large language models. I like research that connects foundational AI to the people who actually use it.

I'm into science, language, and history.
