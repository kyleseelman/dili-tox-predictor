Stress — the accent mark (τόνος)

Every Greek word of two or more syllables carries one written accent on the vowel you stress, and the transliteration copies it: γιατί [yatí]. Stressing the wrong syllable is the quickest way to be misunderstood, so read the accent every time.

  • One-syllable words usually have no accent: με, σε, το, δεν, τι, να. πού “where” keeps its accent.
  • Most verbs stress the syllable just before the ending: θέλω, περιμένει. A group stresses the ending itself: προσπαθώ, μπορείς.
  • Questions are written with ; — the Greek question mark — and made purely with intonation.
